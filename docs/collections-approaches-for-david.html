Collections — what aging means + how other businesses do it

QuickBooks gave us the shape (group by days overdue). This pack explains every bucket in Paddock English, then shows three real-world approaches property / A/R teams use — so we can pick what fits yards, COI, ACH, and ops — not a grey QB clone.

1. Aging buckets — plain English (Paddock)

Aging = how many days past the invoice due date the open balance still sits unpaid. Same idea as QB A/R Aging Detail; buckets are tighter (15 / 30) because truck-yard rent moves faster than 30/60/90 corporate terms.

Bucket Meaning Typical ops move Paddock example
Current Not late. Due today or still in the future (aging = 0). Watch list / soft reminder if ACH failed or invoice just sent. Fisk & Sons — due Nov 15, $200.79 open, not overdue yet.
1 – 15 1 to 15 days past due. Early delinquency. First email / text. Most oversights clear here. AA& D — 8 days late, $232.65, email already logged.
16 – 30 16 to 30 days past due. Still recoverable with a call. Direct call; confirm intent; note promise date. Golden Horn — 30 days, $4,462, “Promised Fri”.
31 – 60 31 to 60 days past due. Survived first reminder cycle. Escalate tone; owner awareness; plan or notice path. ND Asset / ValleyWide — mid bucket, larger balances.
> 60 More than 60 days past due. Highest cash / lockout risk. Formal path: notice, cure, lockout review per lease. Truck Parking Source / Lucas — 100+ days, review lockout.

Other columns on the live page

  • Due date — when that open charge was due.
  • Aging — day count past due (drives the bucket).
  • Open balance — $ still owed (not original invoice if partial).
  • Stage — ops outreach: Communicated · Waiting · Waiting for payment · Payment made (David’s language, not the old A/B/C board).
  • Follow-up note — free text next step (“Promised Fri”).
  • Log call — records that ops called; does not dial the phone.

2. How other businesses structure collections

Patterns from property ops + A/R tools: Tenant Ledger, Scaalr (PM aging), Manetrax, ClearReceivables, Upflow. Common thread: aging for money truth + dated next action for work — not kanban for its own sake.

Act early (day 5 beats day 60) Start oldest / largest first Bucket totals week-over-week Promise-to-pay with a date Shared activity log
